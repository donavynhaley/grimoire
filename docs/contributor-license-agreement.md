# Grimoire Contributor License Agreement

## Why this exists

Grimoire is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, and the
`packages/grimoire-mcp` package under the MIT License. Both of those are grants
made by the copyright holder, and they can only be made by whoever holds the
copyright in the code.

The moment a second person contributes, the copyright in Grimoire is split
between them. Nothing about the AGPL stops working — but every later decision
about licensing does, because it now needs the agreement of everybody who ever
landed a patch. Adding a dual-licensed commercial edition, moving a file into
the MIT package, or relicensing to answer a problem nobody has thought of yet
would each mean tracking down every past contributor and asking permission.

This agreement is how that stays possible. You keep the copyright in what you
write. You give the project a licence broad enough that it can keep making
licensing decisions with one signature instead of a hundred.

It is deliberately a **licence, not an assignment**: your work stays yours, and
you can use it anywhere else you like, including in your own projects under your
own terms.
